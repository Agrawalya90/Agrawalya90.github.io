Realty AI Agent + CRM

An AI sales assistant for real-estate brokerages in the US and Canada. It answers buyers and renters, searches listings, books showings and hands warm leads to licensed agents, inside a full CRM that any brokerage can sign up for.

  • Rules in code, not the prompt. Calling-hour windows per US state and Canadian province, do-not-contact, consent and buyer-agreement checks run before the model can act. Missing data fails closed.
  • Guards on the output. Fair-housing language and false claims ("I've booked you…" with no matching tool call) get one rewrite, then a safe fallback.
  • Multi-tenant SaaS. Each brokerage gets its own workspace, subdomain or custom domain, with automatic https.
  • The whole CRM. Pipeline, inbox, AI phone calls over Twilio, drips, campaigns, MLS sync, e-signing, a hash-chained consent ledger and an Android app.

YTC Billing

GST invoicing, stock and collections for a wholesale business in India. It is built so the totals reconcile exactly, from a single line discount to a partial return on a part-paid invoice.

  • GST done right. CGST + SGST within a state, IGST across states, tax-inclusive pricing and rupee round-off that always reconciles.
  • Exact money. Calculations stay exact where floating-point maths would drift, and a double-clicked submit never saves twice.
  • Real workflows. Payments allocated by hand with advances, returns, cancellations that restore stock, and edits to issued invoices that reconcile everything.
  • Collections. Payment reminders on a schedule that stop when the invoice is settled, plus monthly Excel reports with HSN grouping.
